My Dearest Ayu,

10 October 2024 — Our 2nd Anniversary

I have started writing this letter a hundred times in my head — while commuting, in the quiet pauses between work, and late at night before falling asleep. Yet, every single time, I realize that human words are far too small to hold everything you mean to me.

Two years ago, on the 10th of October, 2024, what started as a simple conversation quietly rearranged my entire world. Before you came into my life, I never knew that happiness could feel so safe, so peaceful, and so effortlessly certain. You became the softest place my tired thoughts could run to, and simultaneously the reason I strive to become a better man every single day.

I look back at these two years — 730 days of shared smiles, silly jokes, fruit juice dates, 3 AM phone calls, and quiet understanding. I remember every laugh that made your eyes crinkle, every moment we shared one plate of food, and every time you playfully pouted. Each of those seconds is folded into my soul like the most precious photograph.

Thank you for your patient heart with me. Thank you for your boundless kindness, for the way you love your family, and for choosing me back — even on the days when I am stubborn or difficult. You have seen me at my best and at my most vulnerable, and you have loved me through all of it.

Today marks our 2nd Anniversary, and I find myself even more deeply, hopelessly in love with you than the day we began. If the universe gave me a thousand lifetimes, I would search for you in every single one, and I would choose you every single time without hesitation.

Happy 2nd Anniversary, my future wife. Thank you for two years of magic. I cannot wait for all the tomorrows we have yet to live.

Forever & completely yours,
Mitu ❤️
